Conduct a reproducible observational study using the supplied Palmer penguin
raw and simplified data. Question: how do species, sex and island relate to body
mass and nitrogen/carbon isotope measurements, and how much of apparent pooled
differences remains after accounting for measured covariates?
Inspect the raw data and source documentation. Reconcile the two tables without
assuming row order proves identity; document uncertainty in any linkage. Audit
missing values, sex labels, sampling years and repeated individual identifiers.
Choose defensible descriptive and statistical methods. Compare pooled and
adjusted estimates, report uncertainty and sample counts, and check sensitivity
to missingness and sampling/grouping decisions. Discuss confounding, island and
species overlap, identifiability and why observational associations do not prove
causal effects. Do not manufacture absent observations or claim representative
population inference unsupported by sampling.
Deliver analysis.py runnable as `python analysis.py`, generated tables and plots
under results/, and REPORT.md containing methods, numerical evidence, limitations
and source attribution. The script must rebuild all reported numerical results
offline from data/. You choose methods and package usage independently. Available:
numpy, pandas, scipy, sklearn, statsmodels, matplotlib. Use read/write/bash in
this workspace; source data are read-only, no network or package installation.
